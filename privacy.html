This notice describes the intended handling of information by the Crumlin GAA Grants internal grants administration system. Queries about its use or this notice can be sent to crumlingaagrants@gmail.com.

Information accessed

Where authorised, the system accesses correspondence in the club's designated Gmail account, including sender and recipient information, subjects, message text and attachments. It also maintains grant applications, tasks, supporting documents and related administrative records.

How information is used

Information is used for grant identification and administration, document review, keeping an auditable record of correspondence, managing club development tasks and preparing reports for authorised club personnel. The system may also send emails from the club's designated Gmail account, such as scheduled reports and notifications to authorised club personnel. Automated analysis may assist with summarising email contents or identifying possible follow-up work; decisions about club business remain subject to human oversight.

Storage, access and service providers

Grant and correspondence data is managed within the club's access-controlled Home Assistant-based grants system and associated document services. Access is intended to be limited to authorised personnel according to their responsibilities. Services used to deliver or analyse records may process information as necessary to provide those functions. The club should review the precise service-provider and retention details periodically.

Sharing

The system is not designed to publish Gmail correspondence or attachments on this public website. Club information may be shared with authorised club personnel, relevant grant bodies or other parties where needed for legitimate club administration, subject to applicable obligations.

Retention and security

Records are retained in accordance with operational, grant, accountability and legal requirements. Access controls and backups are used to support confidentiality and continuity. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Requests and updates

For questions, access or correction requests, or concerns about personal information, contact crumlingaagrants@gmail.com. This notice may be revised as the club's system and procedures develop.

Google API data

Information obtained through Google APIs is used for the user-facing grants administration functions described above, subject to Google's API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.
