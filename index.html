About this application

Crumlin GAA Grants is an internal club administration system supporting grant research, applications, development tasks, associated documents and authorised club communications.

The system is operated for the benefit of Crumlin GAA Club. It is not a public membership application. Access to its administration features and records is restricted to authorised users.

Use of Google services

The application connects to a designated club Gmail account to retrieve relevant messages and attachments for grants administration, review and record keeping. It may also send emails from that account, such as scheduled reports to authorised club personnel. Information may be processed by automated assistance features to help identify relevant correspondence, documents and possible follow-up actions. Human review remains part of the club's workflow.
